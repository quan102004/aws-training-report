### Week 1 Achievements:

* Got acquainted with the culture, rules, and successfully connected with members of the First Cloud Journey (FCJ) community.
* Grasped basic concepts of cloud computing and the 4 core service groups on AWS: Compute, Storage, Networking, and Database.
* Successfully set up an AWS Free Tier account, explored cost optimization methods, and completed required articles/practices to receive $100 credit.
* Became familiar with and proficiently used the two primary AWS management tools:
  * **AWS Management Console**: Intuitive web interface to find and utilize services.
  * **AWS CLI**: Installed, configured credentials (Access Key, Secret Key, Default Region), and executed basic commands.
* Understood and practiced identity management with AWS IAM (Identity and Access Management):
  * Grasped the concept and importance of identity management.
  * Practiced creating IAM Users, IAM Groups, and managing permissions following the principle of least privilege.
